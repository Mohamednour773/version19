Why this module?

In Egypt, Saudi Arabia, and the GCC, post-dated checks remain the backbone of B2B transactions. This module brings every step — from receipt and deposit to clearance, bouncing, settlement, and printing on actual bank check paper — into a single, audit-ready workflow.

Core Capabilities

Everything your accounting team needs to handle PDCs professionally

01

Complete Check Lifecycle

Received, issued, and guarantee checks with full state tracking from draft through clearance, bouncing, settlement, and cancellation.

02

Automated Accounting

Every workflow step posts the correct journal entries automatically. Cancellations reverse cleanly without breaking audit trails.

03

Bank Check Printing

Print directly on bank check paper using configurable coordinates per layout. Includes 6 default banks for EG and SA. Add custom layouts without coding.

04

Bilingual Reports

Aging report, partner statement, due soon, under collection, bounced — all in Arabic and English with proper RTL support.

05

Bounce Management

Track reasons, bank charges, and settlements. Auto-block partners after configurable bounce threshold. Multi-source charges accounting.

06

Smart Reminders

Three-tier email reminders before due date. Daily cron jobs for overdue detection and check-book low-stock alerts.

07

Audit Trail

Immutable operations log records every state change with user, timestamp, and journal entry references. Compliance-ready.

08

Multi-Currency

Handle EGP, SAR, USD, and any currency. Amount-in-words generation in Arabic and English. Hijri date support for Saudi market.
